Digital Safety for Small Vendors

Learn simple practices to protect your digital payments, accounts and business information.

Important Safety Practices

🔐 Protect Your PIN

Never share your UPI PIN, password or other confidential authentication information.

📩 Beware of Scams

Be careful with unexpected messages, links and payment requests.

📱 Secure Your Phone

Use a screen lock and keep your phone and applications updated.

💳 Check Payments

Always verify the recipient, amount and actual payment status.

🔗 Check Links

Do not open suspicious links or provide confidential information on unknown websites.

🚨 Report Problems

If you notice suspicious activity, contact the appropriate bank or service provider promptly.

Remember

🛡️ Simple Rule

  • Never share OTPs or UPI PINs.
  • Verify payment details before approving.
  • Do not trust payment screenshots alone.
  • Use official and trusted applications.
  • Keep important account information private.
