Digital Payment Safety

Staying safe online is an important part of using digital payments. Learn simple practices that can help vendors protect their payment information.

⚠️ Remember

Your UPI PIN is used to authorize certain transactions. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, password or other confidential banking information with another person.

Essential Safety Practices

Follow these basic precautions while accepting or making digital payments.

🔐 Never Share Your UPI PIN

Keep your UPI PIN private. Banks and genuine support services should not ask you to disclose your confidential PIN.

🔢 Protect Your OTP

Never share an OTP received on your phone with another person, even if they claim to be from a bank or payment service.

💰 Verify Every Payment

Vendors should check the actual transaction status before considering a payment completed. Do not depend only on a screenshot sent by someone.

🔗 Be Careful With Links

Avoid opening suspicious links received through unknown messages, emails or social media.

📱 Use Official Applications

Download applications from trusted official app stores and keep your applications updated.

👀 Check Payment Details

Before confirming a transaction, carefully check the recipient and amount shown on the payment screen.

Common Warning Signs

🚨

Urgent Requests

Be cautious when someone pressures you to make a payment immediately.

📸

Fake Screenshots

A payment screenshot can be edited or misleading. Always verify the actual transaction status.

🔑

Secret Information

Never provide your PIN, OTP, password or other confidential information to strangers.

If You Notice a Suspicious Transaction

Stop interacting with the suspicious person or request. Keep records of the transaction and contact your bank or the relevant payment service through its official support channel as soon as possible.
