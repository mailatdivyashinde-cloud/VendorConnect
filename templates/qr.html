QR Payments for Small Vendors

QR codes provide a simple way for customers to make digital payments using their mobile phones. Small vendors can display a QR code and accept digital payments conveniently.

What is a QR Code?

QR stands for Quick Response. A QR code is a two-dimensional code that can be scanned using a compatible mobile phone or payment application. In digital payments, customers can scan a vendor's payment QR code to start a transaction.

📱

Easy to Scan

Customers can scan a payment QR code using a compatible payment application.

⚡

Quick Payments

QR-based payments can make transactions convenient and reduce the need for cash handling.

🏪

Useful for Vendors

Vendors can display their payment QR code where customers can easily see and scan it.

How Does QR Payment Work?

Step 1: The customer opens a compatible digital payment application.
Step 2: The customer selects the scan QR option.
Step 3: The customer scans the vendor's payment QR code.
Step 4: The customer checks the recipient and enters the correct amount.
Step 5: The customer authorizes the payment through the required authentication method.
Step 6: The customer and vendor should check the payment status before considering the transaction complete.

Benefits of QR Payments

💰

Less Cash

Digital payments can reduce the need to handle physical cash for every transaction.

😊

Customer Convenience

Customers can use their mobile devices to make digital payments conveniently.

📊

Transaction Records

Digital transactions can provide records that help vendors track their payments.

QR Payment Safety Tips

🛡️ Stay Safe

  • Always check the recipient and amount before approving a payment.
  • Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.
  • Do not approve unexpected payment requests.
  • Do not trust screenshots as proof of payment.
  • Check the actual payment status in the payment application or account.
  • Use trusted and official payment applications.
