Small Vendor Digital Guide

A simple step-by-step guide to help small vendors understand and start using useful digital tools for everyday business activities.

Start Your Digital Journey

You do not need to use every digital tool at once. Start with simple tools and gradually become more comfortable with digital technology.

1

Understand Your Smartphone

Learn basic functions such as connecting to the internet, installing applications, using the camera and managing important settings.

2

Learn About Digital Payments

Understand how digital payments work and learn about UPI, payment applications and QR-based payments.

3

Start Using UPI

Learn how UPI payments work and understand important information such as payment details, transaction status and UPI PIN safety.

4

Use a Payment QR Code

A suitable payment QR code can provide customers with a convenient way to make digital payments.

5

Explore Useful Digital Apps

Explore suitable applications for payments, customer communication, record keeping, expenses and inventory management.

6

Learn Digital Safety

Protect your UPI PIN, OTP, passwords and confidential information. Be careful with suspicious links and payment requests.

7

Organize Your Business

Use suitable digital tools to organize payment records, expenses, inventory and other routine business information.

8

Build Digital Confidence

Practice regularly, learn from reliable sources and use only the digital tools that are genuinely useful for your business.

Quick Digital Checklist

✔ Smartphone is protected with a screen lock.

✔ Payment application is downloaded from a trusted source.

✔ UPI PIN is kept private.

✔ OTP and passwords are never shared.

✔ Payment status is checked after transactions.

✔ Suspicious links and requests are avoided.

✔ Important business records are organized.

✔ Digital tools are used according to business needs.

Go Digital with Confidence

Digital transformation is a gradual process. Small vendors can begin with simple payment and business tools and learn additional digital skills over time. The goal is not to use technology unnecessarily, but to use useful digital tools safely and confidently.
