Digital Guide for Small Vendors

A simple guide to help vendors understand and start using useful digital tools.

Start Your Digital Journey

Step 1 — Get a Smartphone

Use a suitable smartphone with internet access and basic security features.

Step 2 — Choose Digital Payments

Learn about UPI and supported payment methods that can be useful for your business.

Step 3 — Display a QR Code

If you accept QR-based payments, display the payment QR code where customers can easily see it.

Step 4 — Explore Useful Apps

Choose trusted applications that support communication, records, payments or other business needs.

Step 5 — Learn Digital Safety

Protect your PINs, passwords and other confidential information.

Step 6 — Check Every Transaction

Verify the payment status and transaction details before completing a sale.
