Project Aim

The main aim of VendorConnect is to create a simple online awareness platform that helps small vendors understand and adopt useful digital tools. The project focuses on digital payments, QR codes, useful applications, digital safety and basic business awareness.

About VendorConnect

VendorConnect is a CEP project focused on promoting digital awareness among small vendors. The platform provides simple information about UPI, QR payments, digital applications, business benefits and safe digital practices. The goal is to make digital technology easier to understand and use for everyday business activities.

🎯 Objectives

To spread awareness about digital payments, useful digital applications and safe practices among small vendors.

👥 Target Users

The platform is designed mainly for small vendors and business owners who want to understand basic digital tools.

💡 Need for Awareness

Simple digital awareness can help vendors understand technology, make informed choices and use digital tools more confidently.

🌐 Online Platform

VendorConnect is developed as an online, code-based educational platform without requiring an offline survey.

📚 Educational Purpose

The website provides educational information and practical guidance about digital tools for small business activities.

🔒 Safety Focus

The project also promotes responsible digital usage by explaining basic precautions for protecting confidential payment information.

Technologies Used

VendorConnect is developed using simple web technologies so that the platform can be accessed through a web browser.
