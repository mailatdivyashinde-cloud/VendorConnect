QR Payment Guide

QR codes provide a convenient way for customers to make digital payments by scanning a code using a supported payment application.

What is a QR Code?

A QR code is a two-dimensional code that can store information. In digital payments, a payment QR code can help customers access the payment details of a vendor quickly.

📷 Easy to Scan

Customers can scan the displayed payment QR code using a compatible mobile application.

⚡ Quick Process

Scanning a payment QR can make it convenient to enter or access payment details.

💳 Digital Payment

QR codes can support digital payment methods and reduce dependence on cash-only transactions.

How Does QR Payment Work?

A typical QR payment can follow these simple steps.

Step 1 — Vendor Displays the QR Code

The vendor keeps the payment QR code in a visible and convenient location.

Step 2 — Customer Opens a Payment App

The customer opens a supported digital payment application.

Step 3 — Customer Scans the QR

The customer uses the application's scanner to scan the vendor's payment QR code.

Step 4 — Enter and Verify Amount

The customer enters the payment amount and checks the payment details carefully.

Step 5 — Confirm Payment

After completing the transaction, the vendor should verify the actual payment status.

Benefits for Small Vendors

💰 Less Cash Handling

Digital payments can provide an alternative to handling physical cash.

🤝 Customer Convenience

Customers can have another convenient option for making payments.

📊 Transaction Records

Digital transactions can provide records that may help with basic payment tracking.

QR Payment Safety Tips

✔ Display only the correct payment QR code.

✔ Check the payment status after a transaction.

✔ Do not rely only on screenshots as proof of payment.

✔ Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.

✔ Be careful with unknown or suspicious QR codes.
